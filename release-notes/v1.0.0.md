In adversarial bandit convex optimisation on a convex body of dimension d that contains a unit ball and has diameter D, an explicit learner, and so the minimax regret over n rounds, has expected regret at most a constant times d^{3/2} sqrt(n) ln^{3/2}(nD); the best bound known had d^{5/2}.
