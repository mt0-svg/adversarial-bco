One program of a random access machine with logarithmic cost and a weak membership oracle for adversarial bandit convex optimisation: on every convex body given by the oracle and two rational balls about a known centre, against an adversary that chooses each loss from the whole past, the earlier random bits of the learner included, and with a noise of mean 0 and E e^{ε²} ≤ 2 given the past and the current draw, it plays each round in a number of bit operations polynomial in d, ln n and the bit length of δ, of the centre and of the radii, and its regret is at most a constant times d^{3/2} sqrt(n) ln^5(2dn/δ) with probability at least 1 − δ (Theorem 1.2); on the same bodies, for d ≥ 4·10^6 and n ≥ d^{10}, every learner has regret at least 7·10^{-9} d^{3/2} sqrt(n)/ln d in mean and 2.8·10^{-9} d^{3/2} sqrt(n)/ln d with probability at least 1/2 against some adversary without noise (Theorem 1.3), and the two bounds hold together for the program of Theorem 1.2 (Corollary 1.4).
