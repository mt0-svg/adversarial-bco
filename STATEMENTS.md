# From the paper to the proof

The table below gives, for each numbered statement of the paper, the Lean declarations that prove it.

Seventeen theorems carry the results of the paper. Their statements are written out from the paper's definitions in nine statement files, and Comparator checks each theorem against a copy of its file:

- `AdversarialBco.theoremConj1UpperExp` and `theoremConj1UpperExp_of_conj1`: Theorem 1.1, and Theorem 1.1 from Remark 1.2, stated in `AdversarialBco/StatementConj1Exp.lean` and checked with `config-conj1-exp.json`;
- `AdversarialBco.theoremConj1Upper` and `theoremPoly32O_of_conj1`: Remark 1.2, and Corollary 8.6 from Remark 1.2, stated in `AdversarialBco/StatementConj1.lean` and checked with `config-conj1.json`;
- `AdversarialBco.theoremPoly32O_holds`: Corollary 8.6, stated in `AdversarialBco/StatementPoly.lean` and checked with `config-poly.json`;
- `AdversarialBco.theoremConj1UpperPoly` and `theoremConj1UpperPoly_of_conj1`: Theorem 1.3 (a), and Theorem 1.3 (a) from Remark 1.2, stated in `AdversarialBco/StatementConj1Poly.lean` and checked with `config-conj1-poly.json`;
- `AdversarialBco.theoremConj1UpperPolyExp` and `theoremConj1UpperPolyExp_of_poly`: Theorem 1.3 (b), and Theorem 1.3 (b) from Theorem 1.3 (a), stated in `AdversarialBco/StatementConj1PolyExp.lean` and checked with `config-conj1-poly-exp.json`;
- `AdversarialBco.theoremConj1Lower`: Theorem 1.4, stated in `AdversarialBco/StatementConj1Lower.lean` and checked with `config-conj1-lower.json`;
- `AdversarialBco.Explicit.theoremExplicit`, `lemmaMarg` and `corollaryExplicit`: Theorem 1.5, Lemma 7.13 and Corollary 1.6, stated in `AdversarialBco/StatementExplicit.lean` and checked with `config-explicit.json`;
- `AdversarialBco.BodySpec.dilTheorem`, `bodyExplicit` and `bodyHExplicit`: Theorem 3.1 and Proposition 2.2, stated in `AdversarialBco/StatementBody.lean` and checked with `config-body.json`;
- `AdversarialBco.corollaryADV`: Corollary 1.6 for measurable learners, stated in `AdversarialBco/Statement.lean` and checked with `config.json`.

The constants of the main results are written into these statements. Every other declaration below is a step of their proofs, including the inequalities between explicit numbers that the proofs use. Everything rests on the axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` alone, and `code/formal-proof/main_axioms.lean` prints the axioms of the main theorems. Section 11.2 of the paper lists the places where the formal proof departs from the text.
